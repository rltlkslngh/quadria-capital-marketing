# Marketing of Financial Services — A Case Study on Quadria Capital

> How does a private equity firm managing US$4.3B+ market itself with **zero social media presence**? A first-hand interview with a Partner at Quadria Capital, analyzed through PESTLE, SWOT, and the 7Ps of marketing.

## Why this project

Most "marketing of financial services" coursework defaults to retail products — mutual funds, insurance, SIPs — because that's where marketing looks familiar: ads, apps, campaigns. This project instead looks at the **institutional / B2B side** of financial services, where a firm can manage billions of dollars in assets while running no digital marketing at all.

Through a live interview with **Sunil Thakur**, Partner at Quadria Capital, this project explores how a healthcare-focused private equity firm builds credibility, sources deals, and raises capital purely through reputation, sector specialization, and reference networks — and what that means for how we should actually teach and apply marketing frameworks in institutional finance.

## Key findings

- **No social media, and that's deliberate.** Digital marketing is built for retail audiences; institutional finance runs on conferences, LP roadshows, and referrals.
- **The previous deal is the marketing campaign.** In a reference-driven market, reputation compounds the same way capital does.
- **Sector focus is a moat, not a limitation.** A decade-plus of exclusive healthcare focus builds founder trust, proprietary deal flow, and LP differentiation that a generalist fund can't easily replicate.
- **People are the product.** Quadria's partners combine clinical, financial, and operational expertise — in the 7Ps framework, "People" and "Physical Evidence" (track record, exits) carry more weight than "Promotion."
- **Not everything is measured quantitatively.** Asked what metrics the firm uses to track its "marketing" strategy, the answer was direct: there isn't a specific metric for this. Reputation doesn't reduce cleanly to a dashboard.

## Frameworks applied

| Framework | Used to analyze |
|---|---|
| **7Ps of Marketing** | How each classic marketing element (Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, Physical Evidence) maps differently onto a B2B institutional model vs. retail finance |
| **PESTLE** | The macro-environment shaping healthcare private equity in Asia (political, economic, social, technological, legal, environmental) |
| **SWOT** | Quadria Capital's specific competitive position — strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats |

## About Quadria Capital

Quadria Capital is a Singapore-based, independent private equity firm founded in 2012 by Abrar Mir and Dr. Amit Varma. It focuses exclusively on growth-capital healthcare investments — hospitals, pharmaceuticals, diagnostics, medical devices, and health-tech — across South and Southeast Asia, managing over US$4.3 billion in assets from offices in Singapore and New Delhi. A notable portfolio example referenced in this project is AIG Hospitals, Hyderabad.

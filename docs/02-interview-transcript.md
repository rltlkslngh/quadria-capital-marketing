### About Quadria Capital

Quadria Capital is a Singapore-based private equity firm founded in 2012 by Abrar Mir and Dr. Amit Varma. The firm focuses exclusively on healthcare investments across South and Southeast Asia, with offices in Singapore and New Delhi. Quadria provides growth capital and strategic guidance to healthcare companies such as hospitals, pharmaceutical firms, diagnostics providers, and health-tech platforms. Its partners and investment team combine medical, operational, and financial expertise to scale healthcare businesses. Through its investments, Quadria aims to expand healthcare access, improve quality and affordability of medical services, and strengthen healthcare systems, impacting millions of patients across emerging Asian markets while delivering strong investor returns.

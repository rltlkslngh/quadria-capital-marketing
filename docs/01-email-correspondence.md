### Pre-shared talking points

**Q1. Career trajectory, experience, time at Quadria, and a typical day as a Partner**
- Started career around 2000 on the treasury side with PNB Gilts.
- Moved into advisory, working on project finance, capital markets, and M&A advisory.
- Over time, focus shifted toward healthcare transactions and eventually healthcare investing.
- Today, work centers on investing and helping build healthcare businesses across emerging Asian markets.
- As a Partner at Quadria, the role spans both strategic firm-level responsibilities and investment work.
- A typical day includes reviewing new opportunities, interacting with founders and management teams, guiding the team on diligence and investment memos, and working with portfolio companies on value creation.
- Also spends time engaging with investors and staying connected with the broader healthcare ecosystem.

**Q2. Why focus exclusively on healthcare rather than diversifying?**
- Quadria was built on deep healthcare expertise.
- The founding team came from different parts of the healthcare ecosystem and saw a large opportunity emerging in Asian healthcare markets.
- The idea was to combine sector knowledge with long-term capital to support the growth of healthcare businesses.
- Healthcare offers a rare combination of large market size, long-term demand visibility, and meaningful social impact.
- In Asia, demand drivers are strong: rising incomes, ageing populations, increasing chronic diseases, and expanding insurance coverage.
- Because healthcare is a complex sector, specialization allows the firm to understand its nuances and support companies more effectively.
